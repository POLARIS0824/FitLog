Liquid Glass rendering and navigation adapted from Myspoti at
320ba2a8fef4019b6c9da85c7f618399c6e4064a (https://github.com/kota-zo/myspoti).
Myspoti adapts BitChord edc1e6f (https://github.com/kushagrasinghx/BitChord)
and Echo-Music (https://github.com/EchoMusicApp/Echo-Music).
LiquidGlass.kt, FloatingTabBar.kt and GlassSelectionPill.kt retain GPL-3.0-or-later
headers. See liquid-glass-GPL-3.0.txt for the license.
FitLog modifications: package and logging adapters; native FitLog destinations,
accessibility tab semantics, and DataStore-backed opt-in look.

FloatingTabBar v1.0.1 by Elyes Mansour
https://github.com/elyesmansour/compose-floating-tab-bar
Apache License 2.0 (backdrop-Apache-2.0.txt).

The selection pill's motion follows liquid_glass_easy 4.3.4
https://pub.dev/packages/liquid_glass_easy
